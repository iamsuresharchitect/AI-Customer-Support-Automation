# ShopNest Shipping Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

## Processing time
- Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on a business day are packed and handed to the courier the same day.
- Orders placed after 2:00 PM IST, or on Sundays and public holidays, are dispatched the next business day.

## Delivery options and timelines
| Option | Cost | Delivery time after dispatch |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Free on orders above ₹999, otherwise ₹79 | 3 to 5 business days |
| Express | ₹149 | 1 to 2 business days |
| Same-Day | ₹249 (Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR only) | Same day if ordered before 11:00 AM |

Remote pin codes (parts of the North-East, Andaman & Nicobar, Ladakh) can take up to 3 extra business days.

## Tracking your order
- A tracking link is emailed and sent by SMS as soon as the order is dispatched.
- You can also track any order under **My Account > Orders > Track**.
- Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan after dispatch.

## Late or delayed orders
- An order is considered **late** if it has not arrived 2 business days after the latest date in its delivery estimate.
- For a late order, contact support with your order ID. We open a trace with the courier within 24 hours and update you within 48 hours.
- If the courier cannot locate a late order within 5 business days of the trace, we will either reship it for free or issue a full refund, whichever the customer prefers.
- If a paid Express or Same-Day order arrives late, the shipping fee is refunded automatically within 7 days.

## Lost or "delivered but not received"
- If tracking shows "Delivered" but you did not receive the parcel, check with neighbours and building security, then report it within **48 hours** of the delivery scan.
- We investigate with the courier for up to 5 business days and then reship or refund.

## Changing the delivery address
- The address can be changed under **My Account > Orders** until the order is dispatched.
- After dispatch, the address cannot be changed; you may refuse delivery and the order will be refunded once it returns to our warehouse.

## International shipping
ShopNest currently ships only within India.

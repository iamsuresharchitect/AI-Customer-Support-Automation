# ShopNest Product FAQ

Last updated: 1 October 2026

## Warranty
- All electronics and appliances come with the **manufacturer's warranty**, usually 1 year from the delivery date. The warranty period is shown on each product page.
- Within the return window (10 days for electronics and appliances), ShopNest replaces faulty items directly. See the refund policy.
- After the return window, warranty claims go to the brand's authorised service centre. Keep your ShopNest invoice, which you can download from **My Account > Orders > Invoice**.

## Product received broken or not working
Report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos. You can choose a free replacement or a full refund. A replacement is shipped within 2 business days of approval and the broken item is picked up at the same time.

## Sizes and fit
Every clothing and footwear page has a size chart. If the size is wrong you can exchange it once within 30 days.

## Stock and restocks
Tap **Notify me** on an out-of-stock product to get an email when it is back. We cannot reserve items or share restock dates.

## Price changes
Prices can change at any time. We do not refund price differences if an item goes on sale after you buy it.

## Gift cards
- ShopNest e-gift cards are available in values from ₹500 to ₹10,000.
- Gift cards are valid for 12 months and cannot be returned or exchanged for cash.
- Each order can buy up to 10 gift cards.

## Payment methods
We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, ShopNest Wallet and Cash on Delivery (on orders up to ₹10,000).

## Contacting support
Support is available every day from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM IST through chat in the app, or by email at support@shopnest.example. Email replies are sent within 24 hours.

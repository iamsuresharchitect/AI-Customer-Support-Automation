# ShopNest Returns and Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

## Return window
- Most items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery.
- Electronics and small appliances (blenders, mixers, kettles, headphones) can be returned within **10 days** of delivery.
- Items must be unused, in original packaging, with all tags and accessories.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Innerwear, socks and swimwear
- Opened personal care and beauty products
- Gift cards
- Perishable food items
- Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page

## How to start a return
1. Go to **My Account > Orders** and select the order.
2. Click **Return Item**, choose the item and the reason.
3. Choose free doorstep pickup (available on most pin codes) or drop the parcel at any partner courier point.
4. Pickup is usually scheduled within 2 business days.
5. Once the item reaches our warehouse it is inspected within 2 business days.

## Refund timelines
After the return passes inspection, the refund is issued to the original payment method:
| Payment method | Time for money to reach you |
|---|---|
| UPI / ShopNest Wallet | 1 to 2 business days |
| Credit or debit card | 5 to 7 business days |
| Net banking | 5 to 7 business days |
| Cash on Delivery | Refunded to ShopNest Wallet in 1 day, or to a bank account in 5 to 7 business days |

Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the item was damaged, defective or wrong.

## Damaged, defective or wrong items
- Report damaged, defective or wrong items within **48 hours** of delivery, with photos of the item and packaging.
- You can choose a **free replacement** (subject to stock) or a **full refund**, including shipping.
- Replacements are shipped within 2 business days of approval, and the faulty item is picked up at the same time.
- Products under manufacturer warranty that develop a fault after the return window must be claimed with the brand's service centre; see the product FAQ.

## Exchanges
Size and colour exchanges are available for clothing and footwear within 30 days, once per order.

## Billing problems and duplicate charges
- If you see a duplicate charge, the extra amount is usually reversed by your bank automatically within 5 to 7 business days.
- Duplicate or incorrect charges that are not reversed within 7 days are handled by our billing team. These cases are escalated to a human agent.

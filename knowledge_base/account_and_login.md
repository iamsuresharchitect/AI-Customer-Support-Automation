# ShopNest Account and Login Help

Last updated: 1 October 2026

## Resetting your password
1. Open the ShopNest app or go to shopnest.example/login.
2. Click **Forgot password?**
3. Enter the email address or mobile number on your account.
4. We send a 6-digit code by email or SMS. The code is valid for **15 minutes**.
5. Enter the code and choose a new password (at least 8 characters, including one number).
6. You are signed out of all other devices after a password change.

If the code does not arrive within 5 minutes, check your spam folder and tap **Resend code**. You can request up to 5 codes per hour.

## Account locked
- After **5 failed login attempts**, the account is locked for **30 minutes** for your safety.
- After 30 minutes you can try again, or reset your password to unlock it immediately.

## Changing your email or phone number
Go to **My Account > Profile > Edit**. A verification code is sent to both the old and the new contact for security.

## Two-step verification
Turn it on under **My Account > Security**. Each new device then needs a one-time code sent to your phone.

## Suspicious activity or hacked account
- If you see orders you did not place or your details were changed without your permission, reset your password immediately and contact support.
- Suspected account takeover and fraud cases are handled by our Trust & Safety team and are always escalated to a human agent.

## Deleting your account
Request deletion under **My Account > Privacy > Delete account**. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Open orders and wallet balance must be settled first.
